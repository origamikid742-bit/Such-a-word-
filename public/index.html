          How to play

          Take turns changing one shared row of tiles, the board. At least one real word should always fit it, but bluffing is allowed. Get rid of all your tiles to win.

          The tiles

          130 tiles: 98 letters, 26 blanks and 6 removal tiles. Everyone starts with 7. Hands are hidden, but everyone can see how many tiles each player holds.

          A blank stands for any one letter. A removal tile deletes a letter.

          A good board

          A word fits the board when it's the same length, has the same letters in the same places, and a letter for every blank. Words come from the ENABLE word list.

          CAT, COT and CUT fit.
          Nothing fits.

          Your turn: one move in 1 minute

          1. Swap two different tiles on the board.
            →
          2. Replace a letter: put a letter from your hand on a different letter. The old one is discarded and you draw 1.
            →
          3. Play a letter from your hand on a blank. The blank is discarded.
            →
          4. Add a blank from your hand at either end or between two tiles.
            →
          5. Remove a letter with a removal tile (never a blank). Both are discarded.
            →

          You can't pass. If no move is possible, you draw until one is.

          Pick your move by tapping or dragging, then press Play. If time runs out, the move you picked is played for you. With nothing picked, you draw 3 and miss the turn. Miss 3 turns in a row and you're out of the game.

          Bluffing and challenges

          Leaving a board that no word fits is a bluff, and it's allowed. Anyone else can challenge the latest move until the next move is made.

          The challenged player has 30 seconds to name a word that fits. If they do, the challenger draws 3. If not, they draw 3. Either way the round ends, and whoever lost starts the next one. Then everyone sees which words fitted.

          Rounds

          A round also ends when the last blank is covered, or when everyone swaps in a row for a full lap. Each time, there are 30 seconds to challenge the last move first.

          Between rounds, you may trade one tile from your hand for one from the bag. Traded tiles stay face down until the next round starts, then everyone's are shown at once.

          Each round starts with a new board of 4 blanks and 2 tiles from the bag. The first player may challenge it: if anyone names a word that fits, the first player draws 2. If nobody can, the board is rebuilt.

          The bag and the discard pile

          Tap the yellow bag to see the tiles you haven't seen yet. Tap the trash can to see every used tile, face up. The bag isn't shuffled again until it runs out; then the discard pile is shuffled back in.

          Winning

          When you play your last tile, you're challenged automatically. Name a word that fits within 30 seconds and you win! If you can't, you draw 3 and start the next round. If everyone else leaves or is out, you win too.

          Leaving, watching and chat

          Leaving a game is for good: your tiles go face up to the discard pile. People who join after a game starts watch it, and get a seat in the next game if there's room. Everyone can chat. The host can pass the host role on, remove people, and end a game.
